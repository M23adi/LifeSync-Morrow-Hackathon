🧠 Agentic AI Pipeline — How It Works
01 📡

Perceive

Ingest real-time vitals (HR, SpO2), GPS coordinates, and ABHA patient identity from the ambulance edge.

02 🧠

ML model (GradientBoosting, 98.9% accuracy) predicts deterioration: STABLE → WARNING → CRITICAL.

03 🤖

Act

Multi-agent handshake negotiates with hospital agents, routing to optimal facility with available beds.

04 🔐

Secure

Every transaction hashed with SHA-256 and appended to an immutable cryptographic audit ledger.
